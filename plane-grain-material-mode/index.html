Concept

A Material Dialogue

The identity of the collection rests on a deliberate dialogue between two materials, kept visible rather than resolved away. Every piece is made from just two: 5mm aluminum and solid oak. Toggle Plane / Grain above to shift the site to match.

The Plane

Aluminum

Sanded aluminum sheet, finished with wax for a natural look that matures alongside the oak, in a muted, desaturated slate blue/gray. Sharp architectural lines, geometric rigidity, thin profiles.

The Grain

Oak

Rich, warm oak wood. Organic permanence, texture, and structural mass grounding each piece.

Joinery

Precision Mechanical Joinery

01

Toolless Assembly

Aluminum panels slip directly through precision-milled slots in the oak timber — no tools required.

02

No Adhesives

Rigid structural integrity comes purely from geometry, friction, and gravity — no screws, fasteners, or wood glue.

03

Honest Construction

Metal tabs are left visible where they punctuate the wood, turning the logic of the joint into a decorative, functional feature.

Sustainability

Flat-Pack & Circular

FlatShips fully knocked-down — maximizing shipping volume and minimizing transport emissions.
CleanNo permanent glue or fused materials — aluminum and oak separate cleanly for recycling or repurposing.
Materials & Finish

Surface

Every piece is made from just two materials: 5mm aluminum and solid oak.

Both are sanded to a low grit and finished with wax, so the aluminum ages and matures naturally alongside the oak over time.
